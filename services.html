Ambidex R&D

Quick AI builds for Perth small businesses

Small, custom AI tools that take repetitive admin off your team, built in days rather than months. Each build does one job well: answering and sorting enquiries, booking, quoting, or drafting the documents your team writes over and over.

You own your workspace, and the code can be exported to your own GitHub.

What we build

Trades & home services

  • After-hours enquiry and quote intake: asks the questions your office would (job type, urgency, address, photos) and hands the team a tidy job card.
  • Pre-visit quote prep: turns intake answers and site notes into a draft quote with options, ready for the tech to check and send.
  • Service reminders and follow-up: annual service reminders and follow-ups on open quotes.
  • On-site checklists with a PDF report: filled in on a phone, with a branded report for the customer.

Allied health clinics

  • Booking-request assistant: helps patients choose the right appointment type or location, collects details and queues confirmations for reception, alongside your existing booking system.
  • Front-desk FAQ assistant: answers questions about referrals, rebates, care plans and cancellations, using your clinic’s own answers.
  • Report and letter drafting: first drafts of GP letters and progress reports from clinician notes. The clinician always reviews and signs.

Bookkeepers & accountants

Client document-chasing and tax-time checklists that follow up on missing items automatically, plus an internal assistant that answers questions from your own procedures.

Property managers

A tenant enquiry and application pre-screen assistant, plus first drafts of condition reports, rent reviews and owner reports.
